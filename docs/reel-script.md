# Sakshi: Instagram Reel concepts

Short, vertical, hook-first. The joke should be about the *technology*, never
about real victims. Keep captions on, keep cuts fast, and show the real app.

**Format:** 9:16, 1080 x 1920, 24 to 30 fps, 18 to 28 seconds.
**App:** https://sakshi-eta-eight.vercel.app
**Tone:** dry, fast, a little chaotic. Not a corporate explainer.

---

## Concept 1: "POV: you forgot to check in" (recommended)

A mock panic about the app, not about the danger.

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 | Phone in hand, frantic scroll | **POV: you're a journalist and you forgot to check in** | Trending comedic suspense |
| 1.5–4.0 | Phone buzzes; cut to black | **Your Sakshi vault just released.** | Notification ding |
| 4.0–7.0 | Mock chat bubble from editor | **Editor: "I got the files."** | Typing sound |
| 7.0–10.0 | Slow zoom on phone | **Your phone: still the only copy?** / **Not anymore.** | Beat drop |
| 10.0–18.0 | Fast screen recording: seal, check in, countdown, release, unseal | **Seal. Check in. Go quiet.** | Beat continues, whoosh on cuts |
| 18.0–22.0 | Logo + app frame | **Sakshi. Evidence that outlives the silence.** / **Link in bio. Built on Arkiv.** | Music tail |

Why it works: the first line is a relatable "oh no" moment. The payoff is the
product doing exactly the thing the viewer feared, but safely.

## Production checklist

1. **Record the app.** Use the live Vercel demo on a phone, or a desktop browser
   cropped to 9:16. Record a vault with a 10-second interval so the release
   happens inside the clip.
2. **Capture the key beats.** Seal, the countdown ring, the recovery kit, the
   release, the unseal. Three seconds each is plenty.
3. **Edit in CapCut or Canva.** 9:16, 1080 x 1920, 24 to 30 fps. Large captions,
   safe margins, no more than six words per line.
4. **Use trending audio.** Pick a suspense or meme sound from the in-app library
   so the reel is discoverable. Add a notification ding and a whoosh on cuts.
5. **Hook in the first second.** The first frame must be the text, not a logo.
6. **Keep it under 30 seconds.** Cut anything that does not add a laugh or a
   product beat.
7. **End on the product.** Logo, one line, and "link in bio".
8. **Respect the subject.** No real victims, no real names, no fake news
   headlines. The humor is about a phone being a single point of failure.
